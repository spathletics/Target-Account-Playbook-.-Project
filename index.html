How accounts qualify

A U.S. approval in the last six months or an FDA decision in the next quarter, a launch that forces reps to be trained or retrained fast, and no public relationship with Quantified.

How the pipeline moves

Watchlist accounts move to Researching six weeks before a decision, and the page flags any that are late. The first touch goes out before the decision, while launch training is being planned, and again at approval, when budgets are being spent.
